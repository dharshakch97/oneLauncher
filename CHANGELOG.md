# Changelog

## 0.3.0-beta.1

First public beta of oneLauncher for Android TV 10 and later.

- Desktop with an original abstract wallpaper, translucent menu bar and app dock.
- Installed TV app grid, remote focus, app launch and Home return.
- Same-Wi-Fi phone customization with QR pairing and manual-code fallback.
- Wallpaper uploads, editable home name, three time zones and 12/24-hour clocks.
- Approximate-location weather and city search powered by Open-Meteo.
- Separate launcher styles and phone HTML, JavaScript and CSS.
- Private release signing, documented installation and recovery, and GitHub build/release workflows.

Tested on a OnePlus C00 running Android 10. Support on other TV models is unverified.
The public release key differs from the earlier private test builds; those builds
cannot be updated in place with this APK. Restore a working stock Home before
removing a test build. Uninstalling clears oneLauncher's saved settings and wallpaper.
